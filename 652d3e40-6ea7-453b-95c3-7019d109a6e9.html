Notes

Marie Skłodowska-Curie (1867–1934). Polish-French physicist and chemist. Nobel Prize in Physics 1903 (radioactivity, shared with Pierre Curie and Henri Becquerel). Nobel Prize in Chemistry 1911 (discovery of polonium and radium). First woman to win a Nobel Prize; only person to win Nobel Prizes in two different sciences.

Founded the Curie Institutes in Paris and Warsaw. Died of aplastic anemia caused by prolonged radiation exposure. Her laboratory notebooks are still too radioactive to handle without protective equipment.
