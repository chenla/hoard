Solvay 1927

A Hoard demonstration: holons, expression cards, and temporal context

In October 1927, twenty-nine physicists gathered in Brussels for the Fifth Solvay Conference on "Electrons and Photons." The group photograph taken on the steps of the Institut International de Physique Solvay has become one of the most famous images in the history of science. Seventeen of the twenty-nine pictured are or would become Nobel laureates.

This demo uses that conference to show what Hoard can do. Each physicist has a biographical card (the Whole) and, for the most significant figures, a second card showing who they were on that day (the Expression). The holon assembles these cards into a coherent view: the conference as experienced by the people in the room.

What this demonstrates

  • Holons — A named subset of a hord assembled for a specific purpose. The "Solvay 1927" holon groups 32 cards (29 people plus the conference, the institutes, and the photograph) into one coherent view.
  • Expression cards — The same person rendered differently for a specific context. Einstein's Whole card covers 1879–1955. His expression card shows a 48-year-old Nobel laureate arguing against quantum mechanics at breakfast.
  • WEMI for cards — Whole, Expression, Manifestation, Instance applied to knowledge cards, not just bibliographic records. Same identity, different renderings, no duplication.
  • Temporal context — Understanding these people as they were that day, not as Wikipedia remembers them. The relationships, the tensions, the things that hadn't happened yet.

Try it yourself

cd examples/solvay-1927
hord compile
hord holon list
hord holon show Solvay_1927--21
hord export --holon Solvay_1927--21

The cards are org-mode files in content/. The metadata is TSV quads in .hord/overlays/. Everything is plain text in a git repository. No database, no server, no dependencies beyond Hoard itself.
