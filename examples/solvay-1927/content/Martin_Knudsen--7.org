** Notes

Martin Knudsen (1871–1949).  Danish physicist.
Professor at the University of Copenhagen, where he
was a colleague of Niels Bohr.  Known for his
pioneering experimental work on molecular gas flow
at low pressures.

The Knudsen number — the ratio of molecular mean free
path to a characteristic physical length — is named
after him and is fundamental to rarefied gas dynamics.
He also developed the Knudsen cell, used for measuring
vapor pressures.  His work bridged experimental
physics and physical chemistry.
