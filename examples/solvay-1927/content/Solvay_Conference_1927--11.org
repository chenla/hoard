** Notes

The Fifth Solvay International Conference on Physics,
24–29 October 1927, Brussels.  Theme: "Electrons and
Photons."

29 attendees, 17 of whom were or became Nobel laureates.
The conference is remembered as the site of the
Bohr-Einstein debates over the interpretation of quantum
mechanics — debates that defined the philosophical
fault lines of 20th-century physics.

Hendrik Lorentz, president of the conference, died five
months later.  The group photograph taken on the steps
of the Institut International de Physique Solvay has
become one of the most famous images in the history of
science.
