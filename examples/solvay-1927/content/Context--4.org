** Scope Note

The surrounding structure that gives meaning to a
thing — the relationships, constraints, history, and
situation without which a datum is noise rather than
information.  In Hoard, context is not metadata about
content but the primary product: the system exists to
capture, structure, and deliver the context that makes
knowledge actionable.

** Notes

Context is the concept most central to Hoard and least
well served by existing tools.  A note-taking app
captures text.  A database captures records.  A wiki
captures pages.  None of them capture the web of
relationships, temporal conditions, and domain
constraints that make any of those artifacts meaningful.

The problem is not storage but legibility.  A human
expert carries context implicitly — they know that
this paper was written in response to that controversy,
that this data point is reliable only under these
conditions, that this term means something different
in this field than in that one.  When the expert
leaves, the context goes with them.  The artifacts
remain but become progressively less interpretable.

Hoard addresses this through structured context at
multiple scales.@@margin:Hoard@@  At the card level@@margin:Cards@@: scope notes,
relations, and vocabulary terms capture what a thing
is and how it connects.  At the holon level@@margin:Holons@@:
membership, ordering, and expression preferences
capture which things belong together and how they
should be presented in a given situation.  At the hord
level: the full quad graph captures the complete web
of relationships.

The context cloud — the margin cards surrounding an
article in a holon — is the most visible expression.
Each sidenote is a window into a card's context,
inviting the reader to follow a thread deeper.  The
article provides narrative; the context cloud provides
the structured knowledge that supports it.

Context is also what makes AI collaboration productive.
A model without context hallucinates or produces
generic output.  A model with structured context —
cards, quads, vocabulary, holons — can reason about
the material because it has the relationships, not
just the text.  This is the homoiconic move@@margin:Homoiconicity@@: the
context IS the harness.
