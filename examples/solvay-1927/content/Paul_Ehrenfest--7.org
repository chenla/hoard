** Notes

Paul Ehrenfest (1880–1933).  Austrian-Dutch
theoretical physicist.  Succeeded Lorentz as
professor of theoretical physics at Leiden in 1912.
Known for the Ehrenfest theorem, which bridges
classical and quantum mechanics, and for the
Ehrenfest classification of phase transitions.

Legendary for his penetrating questions and his
ability to expose the core of any argument.  A close
friend of both Einstein and Bohr, he played the role
of honest broker in their debates at the 1927 Solvay
Conference.  Struggled with depression throughout his
life and died by suicide in September 1933, a loss
deeply felt across the physics community.
