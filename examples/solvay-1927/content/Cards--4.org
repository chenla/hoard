** Scope Note

The atomic unit of Hoard.  A card is a single org-mode
file with a UUID, a type, and structured sections
(Relations, Scope Note, Notes, References).  Each card
represents one entity — a person, concept, event, work,
organization, or system — and compiles to a set of
quads that capture its relationships.

** Notes

Cards follow the one-entity-per-file rule.  A card for
Einstein is not a card about physics — it is a card
about Einstein.  Physics gets its own card.  The
relationship between them is captured in quads, not in
the card body.

The card filename encodes identity: Title--suffix.org,
where the suffix indicates the card type (4 = concept,
7 = person, 6 = work, 11 = event, etc.).  The UUID in
the PROPERTIES drawer is the canonical identifier; the
filename is for human convenience.

Expression cards extend the WEMI (Whole/Expression/
Manifestation/Instance) model.  A Whole card captures
the full identity of an entity across its entire
existence.  An expression card captures the entity as
it appears in a specific context — Einstein at Solvay
in 1927, not Einstein the icon.  Expression cards
share the entity's UUID lineage but add context-
specific scope notes, notes, and relationships.

The card structure is deliberately minimal:

- Relations :: typed links (PT, BT, NT, RT, USE, UF)
- Scope Note :: one-paragraph canonical definition
- Notes :: extended discussion
- References :: sources with annotations

This structure compiles cleanly to quads and renders
cleanly to HTML.  Nothing is hidden in formatting;
everything is machine-legible.
