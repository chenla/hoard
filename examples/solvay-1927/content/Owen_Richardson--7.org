** Notes

Owen Richardson (1879–1959).  British physicist.  Nobel
Prize in Physics 1928 for his work on thermionic
emission — the discharge of electrons from heated
surfaces.  His research established the Richardson
equation (sometimes called the Richardson-Dushman
equation), which describes the relationship between
emission current and temperature.

Professor at King's College London and later at
Princeton.  His work on thermionic emission laid the
foundation for vacuum tube electronics, which dominated
electrical engineering until the transistor era.
