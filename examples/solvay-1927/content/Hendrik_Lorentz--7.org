** Notes

Hendrik Lorentz (1853–1928).  Dutch theoretical
physicist.  Nobel Prize in Physics 1902 (shared with
Pieter Zeeman) for work on electromagnetic radiation
and the Zeeman effect.  Developed the Lorentz
transformations, which became the mathematical
foundation of Einstein's special relativity.

Served as chairman of every Solvay Conference from the
first in 1911 until his death.  The 1927 conference
was his last — he died four months later in February
1928.  Universally respected, he was one of the few
figures trusted by all sides in the emerging quantum
debates.
