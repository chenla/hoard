** Notes

Auguste Piccard (1884–1962).  Swiss-Belgian physicist
and explorer.  Professor at the Free University of
Brussels.  Best known for his record-breaking
stratospheric balloon flights in 1931–1932, reaching
altitudes above 15,000 meters in a pressurized
gondola of his own design.

Later invented the bathyscaphe for deep-sea
exploration; his son Jacques used an improved version
to reach the bottom of the Mariana Trench in 1960.
At the Solvay Conference he served as a scientific
secretary alongside Henriot.  His tall, thin figure
and wild hair reportedly inspired Herge's Professor
Calculus in the Tintin comics.
