** Scope Note

Polish-French physicist and chemist (1867–1934). Two Nobel Prizes in different sciences — Physics 1903, Chemistry 1911. Pioneer of radioactivity research who discovered polonium and radium. At Solvay, the experimentalist among theoreticians and the only woman in the photograph.

** Notes

Marie Skłodowska-Curie (1867–1934).  Polish-French
physicist and chemist.  Nobel Prize in Physics 1903
(radioactivity, shared with Pierre Curie and Henri
Becquerel).  Nobel Prize in Chemistry 1911 (discovery of
polonium and radium).  First woman to win a Nobel Prize;
only person to win Nobel Prizes in two different sciences.

Founded the Curie Institutes in Paris and Warsaw.  Died
of aplastic anemia caused by prolonged radiation exposure.
Her laboratory notebooks are still too radioactive to
handle without protective equipment.
