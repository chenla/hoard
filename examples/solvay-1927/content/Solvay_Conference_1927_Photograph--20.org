** Scope Note

Group photograph of the 29 attendees of the Fifth Solvay
Conference, Brussels, October 1927.  Seventeen are or
will become Nobel laureates.  Called "the most intelligent
photograph ever taken" — one of the most famous images in
the history of science.

** Notes

Photograph by Benjamin Couprie, taken on the steps of the
Institut International de Physique Solvay, Brussels,
October 1927.  Public domain.

Front row (seated, left to right): Irving Langmuir, Max
Planck, Marie Curie, Hendrik Lorentz, Albert Einstein,
Paul Langevin, Charles-Eugène Guye, C.T.R. Wilson, Owen
Richardson.

Middle row (standing): Peter Debye, Martin Knudsen,
William Lawrence Bragg, Hendrik Kramers, Paul Dirac,
Arthur Compton, Louis de Broglie, Max Born, Niels Bohr.

Back row (standing): Auguste Piccard, Émile Henriot, Paul
Ehrenfest, Édouard Herzen, Théophile de Donder, Erwin
Schrödinger, Jules-Émile Verschaffelt, Wolfgang Pauli,
Werner Heisenberg, Ralph Fowler, Léon Brillouin.
