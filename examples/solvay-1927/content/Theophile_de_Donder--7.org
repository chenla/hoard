** Notes

Théophile de Donder (1872–1957).  Belgian
mathematician and physicist.  Professor at the
Free University of Brussels.  Considered the father
of irreversible thermodynamics — he introduced the
concept of chemical affinity as a thermodynamic
quantity and developed the theory of chemical
thermodynamics that later influenced Ilya Prigogine.

A friend of Einstein, he worked on applications of
general relativity to gravitational theory.  His
major work, /Théorie Nouvelle de la Mécanique
Chimique/ (1920), laid the groundwork for
non-equilibrium thermodynamics.  Largely self-taught,
he rose from modest origins to become one of
Belgium's leading theoretical scientists.
