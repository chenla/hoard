** Scope Note

British physicist (1902–1984). Nobel Prize 1933. Unified matrix and wave mechanics into a single mathematical framework. Would publish the Dirac equation within a year, predicting antimatter. Said almost nothing at the conference — colleagues measured silence in "diracs."

** Notes

Paul Adrien Maurice Dirac (1902–1984).  English
theoretical physicist.  Nobel Prize in Physics 1933
(shared with Schrödinger, for new forms of atomic
theory).  Predicted the existence of antimatter (1928).
The Dirac equation unified quantum mechanics and special
relativity.

Famously laconic — colleagues joked that a "dirac" was
the unit of one word per hour.  Lucasian Professor of
Mathematics at Cambridge (1932–1969), the chair once
held by Newton.
