** Notes

Edouard Herzen (1877–1936).  Belgian physical chemist.
Grandson of the Russian political thinker Alexander
Herzen.  Served as the scientific liaison to the
Solvay family and the Solvay Institutes, playing an
essential organizational role in the Solvay
Conferences.

While not a major researcher himself, Herzen was
crucial to the conferences as an intermediary between
the industrialist-philanthropists funding the
meetings and the physicists who attended them.  His
position reflected the Belgian institutional
framework that made the Solvay Conferences possible.
