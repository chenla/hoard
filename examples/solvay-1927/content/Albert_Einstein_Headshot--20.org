** Scope Note

Portrait of Albert Einstein taken in 1947 by Oren Jack
Turner at Princeton.  The most widely reproduced headshot
of Einstein, showing him at 68 — two decades after the
Solvay Conference and twelve years after the EPR paper.

** Notes

Photograph by Oren Jack Turner, Princeton, New Jersey,
1947.  Originally distributed by the Library of Congress.
Public domain.  The image has been digitally cleaned and
is the standard portrait used in encyclopedic references.
