** Scope Note

The Institut International de Physique Solvay in the Parc
Léopold, Brussels.  This is the building on whose steps
the famous 1927 group photograph was taken.  Built in the
late nineteenth century, it served as the venue for the
early Solvay Conferences on Physics.

** Notes

Photograph of the Institut International de Physique
Solvay, Parc Léopold, Brussels.  Undated, estimated
late 19th to early 20th century.  Source: Ghent
University Library collections.  Public domain.

The building is situated in the Parc Léopold, a park
in the European Quarter of Brussels that housed several
scientific institutions.  The Solvay Conferences on
Physics were held here from the first conference in
1911 through the interwar period.
