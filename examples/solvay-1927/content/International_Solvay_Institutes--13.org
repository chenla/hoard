** Notes

Founded in 1912 by Belgian industrialist Ernest Solvay.
Based in Brussels.  Organizes the Solvay Conferences on
Physics and Chemistry — invitation-only gatherings of
leading scientists to discuss open problems.

The first conference (1911), organized by Lorentz at
Solvay's request, brought together Planck, Einstein,
Curie, Rutherford, and Poincaré to discuss "The Theory
of Radiation and Quanta."  The tradition continues to
the present day.
