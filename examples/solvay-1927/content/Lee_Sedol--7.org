** Notes

Lee Sedol (born 1983 in South Korea).  9-dan
professional, 18 international titles.  Known for
creative, aggressive play and an ability to find moves
that other professionals had not considered.

His match against AlphaGo in March 2016 was watched by
over 200 million people.  He lost four games but won
Game 4 with his own "divine move" — move 78, a wedge
that exploited a blind spot in AlphaGo's evaluation.
DeepMind later confirmed that AlphaGo had assigned move
78 a near-zero probability.

He retired from professional Go in November 2019,
telling the press: "With the advent of AI in Go games,
I've realized that I'm not at the top even if I become
the number one through frantic efforts.  Even if I
become the number one, there is an entity that cannot
be defeated."

His retirement marked a quiet endpoint: the moment a
world-class human practitioner acknowledged that AI
had permanently surpassed human capability in his
domain.
