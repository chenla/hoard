** Notes

Jules-Émile Verschaffelt (1870–1955).  Belgian
experimental physicist.  Worked under Heike
Kamerlingh Onnes at the cryogenics laboratory in
Leiden before returning to Belgium as professor at
the University of Ghent.

Served as the principal organizer and secretary of
the Solvay Conferences for many years, handling the
logistical and administrative work that made the
meetings run.  While his own research in experimental
thermodynamics and critical phenomena was competent,
his lasting contribution was institutional — ensuring
the Solvay Conferences brought the right people
together at the right time.
