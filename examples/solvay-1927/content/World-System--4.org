** Scope Note

Following Wallerstein's nomenclature: a World-System
(hyphenated) is one instance of a large-scale social
system with a lifecycle — implying that there have been
and will be others.  A World System (no hyphen) refers
to the singular global system at any given time.  We
use "World-System" rather than "civilization" because
the latter has become semantically unusable, and because
the hyphenated form carries the essential implication of
plurality and succession that "civilization" has lost.

** Notes

Wallerstein introduced the distinction in /The Modern
World-System/ (1974).  His core argument: capitalism is
not a natural economic order but one World-System among
many, with a beginning, a development, and an eventual
end.  The hyphen signals that this is a category with
instances, not a unique achievement.

Braudel's treatment of "civilization" in the opening
chapter of /On History/ remains the best analysis of
why the word became unusable.  It accumulated too many
meanings — moral progress, cultural achievement,
geographic identity, technological stage — until it
could no longer do precise analytical work.
"World-System" restores that precision.

*** Lifecycle Phases

A World-System passes through three phases:

- *Dawn W-S*: the emergent phase — new institutions,
  new production, new epistemology.  High potential
  energy.  The thing being built is not yet legible
  to the system it is replacing.

- *Day W-S*: the mature phase — institutions are
  established, production is scaled, the paradigm is
  dominant.  The Modern World-System's Day phase ran
  roughly from the Industrial Revolution through the
  mid-20th century.

- *Dusk W-S*: the declining phase — institutions
  ossify, externalities accumulate, the paradigm's
  assumptions no longer match reality.  We are in the
  Dusk of the Modern World-System now.  This is the
  Jackpot.

*** Two Branches

Each World-System has two core branches:

- *CULTURE*: the slow branch — preservation of memory,
  experience, knowledge across W-S transitions.  The
  CULTURE branches of successive World-Systems
  coordinate over very long timeframes, forming the
  CULTURE World System (no hyphen): humanity's
  continuity mechanism.  This is not a shadow
  government or a conspiracy.  It is how humanity
  avoids starting from zero at each transition — the
  monastery preserving manuscripts through the dark
  age, the guild preserving technique, the oral
  tradition preserving cosmology.

- *MIDDLE*: the fast branch — governance, economics,
  production, the everyday business of running a
  World-System.  MIDDLE operates within a single W-S
  lifecycle and does not persist across transitions.

The CULTURE World System is the three CULTURE branches
working together across dawn, day, and dusk.  It
operates at the deepest pace layer — slower than any
single institution, slower than any single World-System.
