** Notes

Demis Hassabis (b. 1976).  Chess prodigy — second-highest
rated player in the world for his age at thirteen.
Designed the video game Theme Park at seventeen for
Bullfrog Productions.  Earned a PhD in cognitive
neuroscience at UCL, studying the neural basis of
imagination and memory.

Co-founded DeepMind in 2010 with Shane Legg and Mustafa
Suleyman, with the explicit goal of solving artificial
general intelligence.  Google acquired DeepMind in 2014
for approximately £400 million.

AlphaGo's victory over Lee Sedol in March 2016 was a
watershed moment.  Go's complexity — more possible board
positions than atoms in the observable universe — made
brute-force search impossible.  AlphaGo had to learn
through pattern recognition and self-play, making it the
first time a machine surpassed human performance at a
task that required what humans call intuition.  Move 37
in Game 2 was the iconic moment: a move no human would
play, which turned out to be brilliant.

AlphaFold (2020) predicted protein structures with
experimental accuracy, solving a fifty-year-old problem
in biology.  It was arguably the most significant
scientific application of AI to date.  Hassabis shared
the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for this work.
