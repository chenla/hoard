Notes

Albert Einstein (1879–1955). German-born theoretical physicist. Nobel Prize in Physics 1921 (photoelectric effect). Special relativity (1905), general relativity (1915). Emigrated to the United States in 1933. Spent the last decades of his life at the Institute for Advanced Study in Princeton, seeking a unified field theory.

One of the most recognized scientists in history. His work on the photoelectric effect helped launch quantum theory, yet he spent much of his later career arguing against its implications — particularly the abandonment of locality and determinism.
